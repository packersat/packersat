Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join this program?
+
Any current student at The Packer Collegiate Institute who is preparing for the SAT. Whether you're a sophomore just starting out or a junior retaking the test, you're welcome.
Is it really free?
+
Yes, completely. This is a student-run, peer tutoring initiative. There are no fees, no subscriptions, and no costs of any kind. Packer students helping Packer students.
When and where do sessions take place?
+
Sessions are held one to two times a week on campus. Exact times and room locations are shared after you sign up. We do our best to accommodate different schedules.
What if I'm already scoring high?
+
Great! We work with students at all score levels. For high scorers, we focus on eliminating small errors, mastering the hardest question types, and perfecting test-day strategy to push you toward a 1550+.
How do I sign up?
+
Just click the "Sign Up Free" button anywhere on this page and fill out the quick form. We'll reach out within a couple days to confirm your spot and share session details.
