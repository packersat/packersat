– 06 · Common Questions

Frequently
asked.

Everything you need to know before signing up. Still wondering something? Email us at hello@packersat.org.

Everything you need to know before signing up. Still wondering something? Email us at hello@packersat.org.

Q.01 Who can join this program? +
Any current student at The Packer Collegiate Institute who is preparing for the SAT. Whether you're a sophomore just starting out or a junior retaking the test, you're welcome here.
Q.02 When and where do sessions take place? +
Sessions are held one to two times a week, on campus. Exact times and room locations are shared after you sign up. We do our best to work around different schedules.
Q.03 What if I'm already scoring high? +
Great – we work with students at every level. For high scorers, we focus on eliminating small errors, mastering the hardest question types, and perfecting test-day strategy to push toward 1550+.
Q.04 How do I sign up? +
Click the "Sign Up" button anywhere on this page and fill out the short form. We'll reach out within a couple days to confirm your spot and share session details.
Q.05 Is there really no cost? +
Really. No session fees, no materials cost, no fine print. This program is run by Packer students, for Packer students, and it's free for every one of them.
